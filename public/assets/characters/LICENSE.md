`cesium-man.glb` is **Cesium Man** from the Khronos Group glTF Sample
Assets repository.

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMan
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original model credit: Cesium / Analytical Graphics, Inc.

The model is loaded with Three.js `GLTFLoader`, cloned with
`SkeletonUtils.clone`, and animated with `AnimationMixer`. Per-instance
material tinting and scene placement are project modifications.

---

## soldier.glb — "Vanguard" soldier (players and bots)

Rigged, textured soldier character from [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/)
(Adobe), as distributed with the official three.js examples
(`examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb`, three.js r170). Mixamo characters and
animations are royalty-free for use in personal and commercial projects,
including games; they may not be redistributed as standalone assets.

## head/ — "Infinite" 3D head scan by Lee Perry-Smith (soldier faces)

Photo-scanned human head (`LeePerrySmith.glb`, colour and normal maps) by
Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities, based on a work at
www.triplegangers.com, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Taken from the three.js examples (`examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/`, r170);
see `head/LICENSE.txt`. In game the scan is trimmed to head and neck, tinted
to several skin tones, and fitted onto the soldier body in place of its helmet.
